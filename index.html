JobMeasure Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · App: JobMeasure (com.stillwavestudio.jobmeasure) · Developer: Stillwave Studio

Summary

JobMeasure works fully offline. The app does not request internet access and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. Stillwave Studio does not collect, receive or share any of your data.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — project names, customer names and phone numbers, job addresses, notes, photos, measurements, estimates, tasks, price list items and your business profile — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Photos and camera

JobMeasure uses the Android photo picker and the system camera. It does not request access to your whole photo library. Photos you choose are copied into the app's private storage. Location (GPS) information is removed from photos when they are imported. The app does not request your device location.

Backups

Automatic Android cloud backup is turned off for JobMeasure, so your records stay under your control. You can create a backup file yourself from Settings. Backup files are not encrypted and contain your customer records and photos, so keep them somewhere safe.

When data leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share or save it: for example, sharing an estimate PDF to an app you pick, or saving a PDF or backup file to a folder or cloud service you pick. That destination handles the file under its own privacy policy. If you email us for support, your email provider handles that message.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project, photo or item at any time, or erase everything at once in Settings → Delete All App Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Children

JobMeasure is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
